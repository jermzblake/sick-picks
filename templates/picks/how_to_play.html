How to Play

Make weekly NFL picks against the line, track your results, and climb the leaderboard.

Viewing game lines

Open Games to see the current week’s slate. Each row shows the matchup, kickoff time in your local timezone, and three market columns: Spread, Moneyline, and Total.

Odds are shown in American format (for example +150 or -110). The numbers you see are the current line snapshot; when you submit a pick, that snapshot is locked onto your pick.

Making picks

Click a market button on a game to submit your pick. You can take up to one pick in each market on the same game — Spread, Moneyline, and Total.

  • Spread — pick a team against the point spread.
  • Moneyline — pick a team to win outright.
  • Total — pick Over or Under the combined score.

Picks close at the game’s scheduled kickoff. Games that have already started do not appear on the slate.

Picks are final

Once you submit a pick, you cannot change it or make another pick in the same market for that game. The slate shows a locked control for markets you have already taken.

History and scoring

Open Picks to review your history by week or the full season, and filter by market. Results stay pending until the game is final.

  • Spread and Total — win = 100 points, push = 50, loss = 0.
  • Moneyline — points equal profit on a 100-point stake at your locked odds (for example -150 pays 50 on a win; +200 pays 200).
  • Cancelled games — picks are void and do not count toward your total.

Leaderboard

Check the Leaderboard to see how you stack up. Standings are ranked by points, with wins as the tiebreaker. Filter by week or full season, and by market, the same way you filter Picks.
